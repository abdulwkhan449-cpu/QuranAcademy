What is The Insight Quran Academy?

The Insight Quran Academy is a USA based modern Islamic education center offering certified tutors for Quran learning, memorization, tajweed, tafseer, and Islamic studies for all ages.

Anyone of any age can enroll in our courses. We offer specialized programs for kids (ages 4-12), adults, and sisters. Our courses are designed to cater to complete beginners as well as those who wish to refine their recitation and understanding of the Quran.
Yes, we have a dedicated team of qualified female Quran tutors available to provide a comfortable and safe learning environment for sisters and young girls. Our female tutors are highly experienced in teaching Tajweed, Tafseer, and memorization.
We provide a comprehensive range of courses including Quran Recitation with proper Tajweed, Quran Memorization (Hifz), Quran Translation and Tafseer, Daily Supplications (Duas), Namaz (Salah) learning, and Basic Islamic Education.
Our online classes are conducted via Skype or Zoom, providing a seamless and interactive learning experience. You will receive one-on-one live sessions with your assigned tutor at a time that suits your schedule.
